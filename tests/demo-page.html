❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?

Visit the login page and click "Forgot password". A reset link will be sent to your registered email address.

When is the fee payment deadline?

The fee payment deadline for the current semester is November 30th. Late fees apply after this date.

How do I access my marksheet?

Go to Results → Download Marksheet. Results are published within 3 weeks of examinations.

Can I change my department after admission?

Department changes are possible within the first two semesters, subject to seat availability and academic performance.
