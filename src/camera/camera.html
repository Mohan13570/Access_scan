💡 How Eye Tracking Works with AccessScan:
  • One deliberate blink (60ms–1200ms): Selects the highlighted webpage control or opens the on-screen keyboard.
  • Two intentional blinks quickly: Exits the virtual keyboard, closes dialogs, or returns back.
  • Hold eyes closed 1.2+ seconds: Pauses the scanner or opens the Emergency menu.
  • Positioning Tip: Face your webcam comfortably so your eyes appear inside the dashed guide box.
  • 🚀 Relay Mode (Recommended): After clicking "Allow Camera Access", click "Start Eye Scanner (Relay to Tab)". Keep this tab open in the background. Blinks detected here will be relayed to the website you are navigating. This bypasses any camera permission issues on websites.
